*Field report by P-06 · November 10, 2026*

During a PC headset test over Link, my partner loaded in. The menu was a blue rectangle with white text, centered in her view.

Then it *fell*.

She tried to intercept it. Instead, she found herself poking the lab floor tiles.

I did not witness any of these events firsthand, but I did hear about them shortly afterwards, in a very dramatic narrative. The solution was simple:

The menu followed the eye anchor, the transform that positions the headset’s viewpoint. The Meta Quest default character rig’s built-in locomotor was still enabled. My partner’s in-game body appeared to hold position, but it was intangible and had gone through the floor, carrying the rig and the menu with it. She disabled the locomotor. The button stopped falling. At her direction, I applied the same change at scene load.

The button had been following instructions.

Those instructions had been faulty.
